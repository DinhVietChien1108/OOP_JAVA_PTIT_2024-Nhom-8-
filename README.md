I . Abstract

Today's remarkable advancement in information technology and its use

in daily life have given many advantages. High precision will be achieved

while saving time and requiring less human effort thanks to the

application of information technology in management and the

development of management systems. The structure of the ticketing

system is likewise a concern within this larger context.

Even if the procedure for purchasing airline tickets has improved, there

are still some restrictions. In light of this, our team embarked on a project

that attempted to use Java's strength and flexibility to overcome the

shortcomings and complexity of current air ticketing systems. The group

performed extensive study and developed a user-friendly system

employing their knowledge and insights.

The report provides an overview of the main characteristics and functions

of the airline ticketing system, including the ability to search for flights,

manage reservations, handle passenger data, process payments, and

manage bookings,... It places a strong emphasis on integrating with other

systems to guarantee accurate and current flight information.

The project report demonstrates the effective design and

implementation of a Java-based airline ticketing system. A sturdy and

effective software application that revolutionizes the booking of airline

tickets was produced by the team's thorough investigation and

application of Java programming principles. The system is a useful asset

for both airlines because of its user-friendly design, extensive

functionality, and integration capabilities.


II. Introduction

Pertaining to airline ticket reservation methods

An airline booking system is a piece of software that controls

tickets, passenger data, and aircraft reservations. It is designed

to give customers a quick and easy way to look up flights,

choose their preferred choices, and make reservations.The air

ticket purchasing system was created to do away with the

challenges that come with using more conventional techniques,

such calling or visiting a travel agency or airline office in person.

The program has been kept as simple as possible to minimize

data entry errors. The user of this technology doesn't need any

special training. All of this demonstrates how approachable it is.
